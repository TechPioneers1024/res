Last updated: November 7, 2026

This policy applies to Flashlight-Torch&Screen Light (also called “Flashlight”; Android package flashlight.torch.screenlight) provided by TechPioneers Lab (“we,” “us,” or “our”). It explains how the app and its service providers access, use, store, and share information, and how you can exercise your privacy choices.

Contact us about privacy at techpioneerslab@gmail.com. Our website is www.techpioneers-lab.top.

The app provides flashlight, screen light, compass, and map features. It includes advertising, analytics, crash reporting, and Google Play purchases. You do not need to create a separate TechPioneers Lab account. Location permission supports the map's location features; it is not needed to turn on the flashlight or screen light.

1. Information we process

Lighting, compass, and local preferences

The lighting features control the device's flash or display. The compass uses device sensor readings to display direction. App preferences, such as lighting settings, are stored on the device. Controlling the flash does not itself take photographs or record video.

Location and maps

If you grant location permission and use the map's location features, the app can access your device's precise or approximate location to show your position and move the map. The accuracy depends on your Android permission settings and available positioning signals. The app uses Android/Google location services and Google Maps. Map requests and interactions can involve location-related information, device information, and network information processed by Google.

You can deny or revoke location permission in Android settings. This limits location-dependent map features; the flashlight and screen light remain available. Location can also appear in local diagnostic logs. If you choose to send a feedback email with those logs attached, the attachments may include that location information.

Device, advertising, usage, and diagnostic information

The advertising and Firebase services listed below may process IP addresses, advertising or app-instance identifiers, device model, operating system and app version, language or region settings, app and ad interactions, and crash or performance information. An IP address may be used to estimate an approximate location. The information processed varies by SDK, app version, service configuration, device settings, and applicable privacy choices.

This includes data processed directly by an embedded third-party SDK, even when we do not receive the underlying raw data ourselves. We receive analytics, diagnostic, and advertising reports through the services we use.

Purchases

Google Play handles payment. The app processes purchase information such as product identifiers, purchase tokens, transaction status, and entitlement information to provide or restore paid benefits and handle refunds or reversals. We do not receive your complete payment-card details from Google Play. If you contact support about a purchase, you may provide an order ID or other transaction details.

Support and feedback

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message, and any attachments you send. The in-app feedback feature may prepare diagnostic logs for attachment and include information about the app or purchase status. Logs may contain device details, errors, app activity, and location information recorded while using map features. Your email application completes the sending step. Review or remove attachments before sending, or contact us directly without using the in-app feedback feature.

Website visits

Our website hosting may record technical access information, such as IP address, requested URL, browser information, and request time, to deliver pages and diagnose availability or security issues. These policy pages do not load advertising SDKs or set advertising cookies. The app's SDK identifiers and any tracking on third-party websites you choose to visit are separate from these policy pages.

Agreeing to the Terms of Service is not blanket consent to optional data processing.

4. Permissions and privacy choices

  • Location: use Android Settings > Apps > Flashlight > Permissions to change location access or choose approximate location where Android offers that option.
  • Advertising: use the choices presented in Google's privacy messages when available in your region. Android advertising settings may also let you reset or delete your advertising ID. These device controls do not stop all data processing or delete previously collected information.
  • Further advertising choices: the providers linked above explain their own privacy controls. You may also contact us to request withdrawal of consent or an opt-out of relevant sharing or targeted advertising under applicable law. Identify Flashlight and describe your request so we can determine the information and services concerned.
  • Paid ad removal: the app offers a one-time ad-removal purchase. Removing ads is not a promise that every other processing activity, such as crash reporting, purchase verification, or a map request you make, stops.
  • Feedback: sending feedback is optional. Review the email and its attachments before sending.

Withdrawing consent does not affect processing lawfully carried out before withdrawal. Other processing may continue where a separate lawful basis applies. If a privacy control you need is unavailable, email us at the contact address below.

5. Storage, retention, and deletion

  • Local app data: settings, cached information, and diagnostic logs are kept on your device until replaced, cleared, or removed. You can clear app storage in Android settings or uninstall the app to remove its private local data. Copies you send through email or another service are not removed by clearing app storage.
  • Analytics, advertising, and crash data: retention depends on the provider, data type, service settings, and purposes described above. Provider policies are linked in section 3. Uninstalling the app does not automatically delete information already held by those providers.
  • Support and transaction records: we retain information as needed to answer your request, resolve the issue, maintain valid purchase benefits, meet applicable record-keeping obligations, or handle disputes. The relevant period depends on the nature of the request or record and applicable requirements.
  • Deletion requests: email techpioneerslab@gmail.com with “Flashlight data deletion request,” the app/package name, and enough information to identify the records concerned. For a support conversation, contact us from the email address used. Do not send passwords or full payment-card details.

We will assess and respond to requests under applicable law and delete or anonymize information under our control when it is no longer needed, subject to lawful retention requirements. We may need proportionate verification before acting. For information a provider controls independently, you may need to use that provider's request process; we will explain this where relevant. The app does not require a separate TechPioneers Lab user account, so deletion of local app data is distinct from deleting your Google account or Google Play purchase records.

6. Security and international processing

Local app data is subject to Android's application storage controls. The Google services used for maps, advertising, and Firebase provide encrypted network connections for their service traffic. Support email is handled by the email providers involved and is not a private in-app messaging channel. Access to information received for support is used for the support, operational, and legal purposes described in this policy. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

We and our service providers may process information in countries other than your own. The providers' policies explain their processing locations and transfer safeguards. Where applicable law requires safeguards for international transfers, those requirements apply to the relevant processing.
